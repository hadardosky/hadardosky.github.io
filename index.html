LET'S LOOK CLOSER

There's more to gaming than meets the eye.

Video games can influence our social lives, physical health, and psychological well-being. But their effects depend on more than just the fact that someone is playing a game. What they're playing, why they're playing, and who they're playing with can all make a difference.

Research suggests that certain aspects of gaming can support well-being. Let's break down three of them.

SO, WHAT'S THE POINT?

It's not about whether gaming is good or bad.

It's about understanding when and how it can affect well-being. Motivation, moderation, social interaction, and the features of a game all play a role.

The game matters. But so does the person playing it.
